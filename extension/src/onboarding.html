Huh?

Missed what someone just said in a video? Press a key and Huh? tells you — transcribed, translated and explained in your language, powered by Sarvam AI.

How to use it

  1. Open a video (YouTube, Vimeo, any tab playing audio).
  2. Press Alt+H once (or click the Huh? toolbar icon). The badge turns green ON — Huh? is now listening.
  3. Missed a line? Press Alt+H again. The video pauses and a card explains the last few seconds.
  4. Esc closes the card and resumes the video. Click the icon again to stop listening.

Your privacy

  • Huh? only listens to a tab after you press the shortcut or click the icon on it, and Chrome shows its “sharing this tab” indicator while it does.
  • Audio is kept in memory as a rolling 25-second buffer. It is never written to disk.
  • A clip is sent to your Huh? server — and from there to Sarvam AI — only when you press the shortcut.
  • The server doesn’t log audio or transcripts; it logs only request ids, latency and status.
